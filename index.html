Ghost Co-op

Your past runs come back as echoes. Team up with yourself to solve each room.

  1. Run the level. Press Save echo (R) when you want your run to replay as an echo.
  2. Echoes repeat your exact moves, press plates, and are solid from above.
  3. Reach the glowing exit with the live player. Fewer echoes = more stars.
